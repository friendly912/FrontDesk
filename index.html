Behind the numbers

Why texting beats calling now

Customers reach for a message before they reach for the phone. Calling a shop mid-task feels like an imposition — texting doesn't, which means reply speed on that first message is doing more of the selling than your storefront ever did.

It shows up hardest in appointment-based trades — salons, clinics, repair shops — where a customer is usually just checking before they commit to anything. Whoever answers that check-in first tends to get the booking. Frontdesk exists to make sure that's always you, even when nobody's within arm's reach of a phone.
